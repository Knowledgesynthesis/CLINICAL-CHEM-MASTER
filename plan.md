A complete, **mobile-first**, **dark-mode**, **offline-ready** educational platform covering **Clinical Chemistry** as taught to **pathology residents** during a **clinical chemistry rotation** or in a standard clinical chemistry textbook.

# **7. CLINICAL CHEMISTRY REASONING FRAMEWORK**

1. **Specimen handling**  
2. **Assay principle** (know how the test works)  
3. **QC status** (is assay performing correctly?)  
4. **Result plausibility**  
5. **Interference check**  
6. **Delta check**  
7. **Pattern recognition**  
8. **Clinical integration**  
9. **Reflex/consult decision**  

Pitfalls:
- Believing all results without QC context  
- Missing hemolysis/lipemia effects  
- Confusing assay interference with true pathology  
- Misinterpreting endocrine assays due to binding protein changes  
- Over-relying on immunoassays without confirmatory tests
